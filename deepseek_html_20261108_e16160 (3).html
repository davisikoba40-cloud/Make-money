Terms, privacy & payouts

Short, plain-language summaries of how Mobilearn works.

Terms of service

Mobilearn is a platform operated by Mobilelabs UG for sponsored YouTube video rewards. Accounts are personal and may not be shared. Signup is limited to Airtel Uganda mobile numbers. One-time activation is UGX 19,500 via Airtel *185*9# using Merchant Code 7285061 (MOBILE LABS). Rewards are paid once per video per account and require the video to be watched in full. Skipping, fast-forwarding or automated viewing invalidates the reward. Misuse, fake screenshots or duplicate accounts may result in suspension.

Privacy policy

We collect only what's needed to run your account: username, Airtel phone number and merchant ID. We never sell your data. Phone numbers and merchant IDs are used for verification and payouts only. Screenshots sent via WhatsApp are used solely to verify your video rewards and merchant ID.

Payout policy

Withdrawals are available from UGX 20,000 to Airtel Money only. You must save a valid Airtel merchant ID (via Dashboard → Payout details) before requesting a withdrawal. Withdrawals are verified before payout, usually within 24 hours. Video rewards require a valid screenshot sent to WhatsApp +256 747 943 247.
